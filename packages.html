Questions

Before you ask

Why aren’t there any prices?

Because a backyard wedding and a 300-guest one aren’t the same job, and pretending they are helps no one. Tell me what you’re planning and you’ll get a clear, fixed quote — no surprises, no upselling.

Who’s actually shooting?

Just me, Eddie. No rotating crew, no juniors sent in my place. If a day genuinely needs a second pair of hands, I bring someone I trust — and I’ll tell you that up front.

How far in advance should I book?

Weddings: 6–12 months for popular dates. Events, food and everything else can usually be sorted within a week or two. A deposit locks the date in.

Do you travel?

Happily. Nearby travel is included; for anything further you just cover the travel costs — quoted up front, no markup.

When do I get my photos and films?

Each package lists an honest window — I’d rather deliver it right than deliver it fast. You’ll always get a sneak peek early so you have something to share.

Do you hand over RAW files?

Not usually — the edit is half the work. If you need the raw archive for your own records, ask and we’ll sort something out.

How do payments work?

A deposit holds your date; the rest is due once everything is delivered and in your hands.

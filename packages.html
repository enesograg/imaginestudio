Questions

Before you ask

Why aren't there any prices?

Because a backyard wedding and a huge one are not the same job, and pretending they are helps no one. Tell me what you are planning and you will get a clear, fixed quote. No surprises, no upselling.

Who's actually shooting?

Just me, Eddie. No rotating crew, no juniors sent in my place. If a day genuinely needs a second pair of hands, I bring someone I trust, and I will tell you that up front.

How far in advance should I book?

Popular wedding dates go early, so reach out as soon as you have yours. Events, food and everything else can usually be sorted within a couple of weeks. A deposit locks the date in.

Do you travel?

Happily. Nearby travel is included, and for anything further you just cover the travel costs, quoted up front with no markup.

When do I get my photos and films?

It depends on the job and the season, and I would rather deliver it right than deliver it fast. You always get a sneak peek early so you have something to share.

Do you hand over RAW files?

Not usually, because the edit is half the work. If you need the raw archive for your own records, ask and we will sort something out.

How do payments work?

A deposit holds your date, and the rest is due once everything is delivered.

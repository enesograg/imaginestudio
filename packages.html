Questions

Before you ask

How far in advance should we book?

Weddings: 6–12 months for peak season dates. Commercial and automotive shoots can usually be scheduled within 2–3 weeks. A signed agreement and deposit hold your date.

Do you travel?

Yes — worldwide. Travel within 50 km is included in every package; beyond that we quote travel and accommodation at cost, with no markup.

When do we get our photos and films?

Each package lists its delivery window. Wedding couples get a sneak-peek selection within 72 hours, so you have something to share while the full edit is in progress.

Do you deliver RAW files?

Not as standard — the grade and the edit are half the craft. If you need the raw archive for your records, it's available as an add-on.

How do payments work?

A 30% deposit secures your date; the balance is due on delivery. For commercial projects we can invoice in milestones to match your procurement process.

Can we adjust a package?

Always. The tiers are starting points — mix photo-only, film-only, add hours, add a second shooter. Tell us what matters to you and we'll shape the package around it.

The studio

Behind the lens

It started with short films. It became a studio.

Imagine began as @imagineshortfilms — one camera, one editor's chair, and an obsession with making small moments feel like cinema.

Today it's a full production studio led by Eddie: weddings told like features, brand campaigns shot for every screen, and automotive films that do justice to the machines. The name grew, the gear grew — the eye stayed the same.

How we shoot

Three rules we never break

/ 01

Story first

Pretty frames are easy. We chase the glance between the vows, the hands on the wheel, the product in real life — the shots that mean something in the edit.

/ 02

Chase the light

We plan every shoot around light — golden hour for couples, hard shadows for products, city glow for cars. If the light isn't right, we wait for it.

/ 03

Made to be watched everywhere

Every delivery works on a cinema screen and on a phone: 16:9 for the big moments, verticals for the feed, stills for print. One shoot, every format.

About Eddie

Behind the lens

It started with short films. It's still just me.

Imagine began as @imagineshortfilms — one camera, one editor's chair, and an obsession with making small moments feel like cinema.

Not much has changed since. It's Eddie, a camera bag and an editing desk — shooting weddings, events, food, cars and homes. No account managers, no handovers: the person you message is the person who shows up, and the person who edits every frame.

How I shoot

Three rules I never break

/ 01

Story first

Pretty frames are easy. I chase the glance between the vows, the hands on the wheel, the plate hitting the pass — the shots that mean something in the edit.

/ 02

Chase the light

I plan every shoot around light — golden hour for couples, a room's own glow for food, city lights for cars. If the light isn't right, I wait for it.

/ 03

Made to be watched everywhere

Every delivery works on a cinema screen and on a phone: 16:9 for the big moments, verticals for the feed, stills for print. One shoot, every format.
